What is BusJatri?

BusJatri (বাস যাত্রী — "bus passenger") is a free, non-commercial bus timetable for West Bengal. We list bus routes, timings and stoppages across the state — SBSTC, WBTC, NBSTC and private operators — so travellers can find the next bus without calling anyone.

  • 2,000+ route pages covering all 23 districts of West Bengal
  • Search by stoppage — from anywhere, to anywhere
  • Bilingual — every page in English and Bengali
  • Nearest railway station shown for major stops
  • Weather at destination on bus detail pages
  • Free forever — no ads clutter, no login, no app install

Our data

Bus timetables change often. We combine publicly available sources with community reports, and we clearly mark the last-updated date on every timetable page. If a timing looks wrong, use the Report Time button on any bus page — corrections reach the team directly.

We are not affiliated with SBSTC, WBTC, NBSTC or any transport corporation.
