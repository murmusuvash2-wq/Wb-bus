Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Overview
BusJatri (wb-bus.vercel.app) is a free bus timetable website. We collect almost no personal data. You can browse every timetable without an account, without a login and without telling us who you are.
What we store on your device
We use your browser's localStorage (not tracking cookies) for two small preferences:
• bj-theme — your dark/light theme choice
• bj-lang — your English / বাংলা language choice
These stay on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes them. You can also block localStorage in your browser settings — the site keeps working with default preferences.
Contact form
The contact form does not send data to a server. It opens your own email app with the message pre-filled — you choose whether to send it. Anything you email us is used only to reply to you.
Geolocation (optional)
The search box can optionally detect your city to pre-fill the "From" field. Your browser asks for permission first. If you allow it, coordinates are sent once to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) to resolve a city name; they are never stored by us. If you deny permission, nothing happens and the site works normally.
Google AdSense
We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
• You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
• You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
• More details: Google's Advertising policies.
Third-party services used by this site
• Google Fonts — loads the fonts used on this site (Google may log requests).
• Open-Meteo — destination weather on bus pages (queries contain a place name only).
• Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — optional city detection (see above).
We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone — we barely have any.
Your rights
Since we do not maintain user accounts or profiles, there is no personal data for us to export or delete. Emails you send us are deleted once your query is resolved.
Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be highlighted on the page.
